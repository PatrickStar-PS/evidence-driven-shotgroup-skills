# Adaptive Evidence Clipped Shot Groups

Run an isolated experimental variant of `clipped-video-to-british-shot-groups`. Preserve the source video, locked dialogue injection, asset names, absolute timestamps, model-authoritative cut logic, and overlap seam rules.

Keep asset-recognition evidence separate from generation prose. Under the current contract, require `fixed_scene_evidence` plus structured `generation_facts`; expand bound person-state assets once per subshot and omit fixed wardrobe, fixed scene dressing, fixed lamps, light strips, chandeliers, neon, and static coloured ambience from all downstream generation fields. Retain only subject-lighting condition, exposure/contrast, time of day, depth of field, focus/composition changes, and narrative light changes such as switch-on/off, flashing warning lights, sweeping headlights, or screen light reaching a face.

Keep visible mouth motion separate from dialogue ownership. Under contract `3.9-mouth-motion-speech-sync`, require `mouth_visual_action`, `speech_sync_status`, and `mouth_action_evidence` for each visible person. Do not infer speaking from a single open-mouth frame, visible teeth, subtitles, or gestures. Only a matching locked on-screen dialogue event plus continuous speech-like mouth motion may produce `mouth_state=speaking`; otherwise preserve the visible mouth action as a reaction or non-speech motion.

Track every handled transient prop with `prop_continuity`: stable `instance_id`, visible instance count, identity invariants, holder, physical contact, start/middle/end state, visible source, adjacent-record inheritance, and exclusive post-handoff holder. Treat flipping, opening, closing, or showing another face/lining as the same instance unless two separate objects are simultaneously visible. Treat body-contact, covering, restraint, concealment, bedding-contact, and worn/held state objects as persistent state objects, not disposable background detail: blindfolds, eye veils, scarves over eyes, wrist ribbons, bindings, blankets, duvets, sheets, pillows actively supporting/covering a person, coats, wounds, blood, wet hair, and handheld objects must remain in `prop_continuity` or character-state continuity until a later frame explicitly removes, releases, uncovers, drops, replaces, or proves them gone. If the object is not visible in a later shot but has not been released or removed, write it as inherited but currently hidden/partly off-frame/needs verification; do not omit it. For a handoff, require one object before transfer, shared contact only during transfer, and exactly one holder afterward with the giver's relevant hand empty. Require a visible source before handoff/use, but write `unclear` rather than inventing one. During local expansion, emit continuity warnings only; never block export, rewrite semantics, trigger a relay retry, or fabricate a missing prop state.

## Required inputs

Require one or more episode videos and, for every episode, the current project's eight-column British asset workbook, validated whole-episode dialogue injection, whole-episode boundary JSON, relay key pool, and authorized TOS configuration. The asset workbook and dialogue ledger must belong to the current project. Copying shared relay or web configuration from another project does not authorize reuse of that project's assets, dialogue, shot groups, review state, or generated outputs. Record the resolved absolute input paths and fingerprints before analysis, and stop when a project-local required input is missing instead of silently falling back to another project's file. Read [references/clipped-transport-contract.md](references/clipped-transport-contract.md), [references/adaptive-evidence-contract.md](references/adaptive-evidence-contract.md), [references/analysis-rules.md](references/analysis-rules.md), [references/output-schema.md](references/output-schema.md), and [references/batch-execution.md](references/batch-execution.md) before a multi-episode relay run or export.

For every standard, formal, combined, canary, or web-adapter export, build `画面内容` only with `scripts/lib/shot_content_contract.mjs::composeShotContent`. Project-specific exporters may change workbook titles, statistics, styling, previews, and column placement, but must not copy, simplify, or independently rebuild the content prose. Stop if the shared composer cannot be imported; never fall back to a local formatter. Record `content_contract_version`, `composer_file`, and `composer_sha256` in the export report. Fail export unless every row passes the shared contract validator, including exactly one ordered `表情/视线：` then `声音/台词：` section and exactly one final-group continuity handoff where required.

Before treating a segmented whole-episode dialogue ledger as validated, run scripts/derive_dialogue_ledger.py into the current run directory and keep the source ledger unchanged. Merge time-overlapping contained or high-similarity same-speaker transcriptions into the most complete event, extend that event to the union of their observed time windows, retain an audit trail of every absorbed ID, and flag every cross-speaker resolution. Snap only residual adjacent overlaps of at most 0.25 seconds. Stop on every larger unresolved overlap. Use the derived ledger and its new fingerprint for every range injection, relay call, dialogue segment, and final validation; never mix the source ledger with a derived injection.

Create one stable model workspace for every planned range under the current run directory: `workspaces/<episode>/<stage>/<range_id>/`. Never share a range workspace across episodes, stages, ranges, source-video fingerprints, or derived-dialogue fingerprints. Keep that range's `inputs`, `evidence`, `request`, `response`, `normalized`, `audit`, `retry`, and `logs` artifacts inside its own workspace; expose the resolved path to subprocesses as `ADAPTIVE_RANGE_WORKDIR`. Use absolute paths in command arguments and let the runner use the range workspace as the process working directory unless a legacy manifest explicitly supplies `cwd`. Record the workspace path and contract fingerprint atomically in batch status. On resume, reuse a completed command only when its command fingerprint and workspace contract fingerprint both match; never recover an artifact by scanning a sibling range or another project.

## Workflow

1. Probe the original episode and detect whole-episode boundary candidates.
2. Plan 8-10 second logical cores with 1.5 seconds of physical clip context. Treat eight ranges as the default soft limit, not a hard ceiling. When duration or boundary density cannot satisfy the 12-second or candidate-density targets within eight ranges, automatically expand to the minimum additional range count needed and record the expansion reason, final range count, and any residual pressure warnings. Materialize clips with `clip_analysis_ranges.py --workers 2 --ffmpeg-threads 3 --resume`; reuse only a clip whose source fingerprint, absolute range, encoding contract, duration, and output fingerprint match its cache metadata. For a canary, materialize exactly one range.
3. Build ordinary OpenCV evidence from the original video to retain `shot_space` and geometry JSON.
4. Run `build_pose_evidence.py` locally to create unmarked `action_events` and `high_motion_triplets` pages. These pages replace dense contact sheets; they do not determine final cuts.
5. Run `../person-trajectory-evidence/scripts/track_people.py` against the original episode and a previously validated or boundary-derived absolute-time range timeline. Reset tracks at every supplied cut. Each per-shot trajectory page must use the shot midpoint as its representative background frame, not the last sampled frame, so the trajectory page stays visually comparable with `shot_space` and pose evidence when a shot contains reframing or push-in motion. Then run `scripts/assemble_trajectory_page.py` to create one `person_trajectory` page for the range. Treat supplied trajectory shot divisions as evidence partitions, not final cut truth. Do not run the tracker against an overlap clip with absolute timestamps unless they have first been converted to clip-local time.
6. Run `score_pose_complexity.py` with the OpenCV manifest and event manifest. Save its auditable score and reasons.
7. When `pose_enabled=false`, skip JointBDOE and MediaPipe fusion. When true, run representative-frame extraction, JointBDOE CPU inference, and MediaPipe per-crop fusion. Treat the overlay as auxiliary geometry only. Do not use JointBDOE in production until its licensing is reviewed.
8. Run `assemble_adaptive_evidence_pack.py`. Always include range boundaries, action events, high-motion triplets, shot space, and one consolidated person-trajectory page. Include multi-person geometry only when the gate enables it.
Before every range call, filter the current project's eight-column asset workbook by the current episode's `出现集号` and inject every matching `人物`, `场景`, and `道具` row. Do not reduce the episode injection to character cards or impose a silent row limit. Label each card by type and include its exact Chinese source name, canonical localized name, and Chinese recognition description. Require every directly observed match to place the exact Chinese source asset name in `asset_hints`, while keeping unobserved assets unbound. Record the injected total and per-type counts in the relay audit.
9. Rebuild every range's dialogue injection from the current validated whole-episode ledger immediately before analysis with `scripts/build_dialogue_injection.py`. Do not depend on a sibling skill path. The injection must contain `source_ledger.sha256`; pass the same ledger through `--dialogue-ledger`. Reject a missing or mismatched fingerprint before any relay request—never reuse an older range injection after the ledger changes. Upload every overlap clip once and reuse its content-addressed transport object for retries and seam audits. Preflight the relay key pool with a short-lived fingerprinted cache. Treat every healthy key as `per_key_concurrency` reusable lanes rather than one worker; default to two lanes per key and cap the shared pool at 30 active relay calls for 15 healthy keys. Run every relay command through `run_with_relay_lease.py`, include `--key-offset {key_offset} --single-key-only`, and use the shared SQLite lease database so different Codex windows cannot occupy the same lane. Use `run_multi_episode_pipeline.py` with three episode workers, eight range workers per episode, and two local-media workers by default. The primary wave has no cross-range continuity injection because same-run preceding outputs do not exist yet. Normalize and validate every primary result independently, retrying a blocking range at most once under a newly acquired healthy key lease. As soon as one episode's selected normalized ranges exist, build that episode's right-hand continuity injections and run its lightweight `bai_audit_range_seam.py --continuity-injection <file>` calls concurrently; another episode may still be in its primary wave. A seam audit may replace only the right-hand range's transport-seam audit and must not rewrite shot semantics. Only when a seam audit exposes a material semantic contradiction may that single right-hand range receive a targeted full rerun with `bai_compact_timeline.py --continuity-injection <file>`. Sequential same-run continuity is opt-in only when the user explicitly requests sequential mode. The runner records episode/stage/command state atomically, resumes only matching command fingerprints, and records the gate decision, worker count, leased key fingerprint/slot, primary-wave status, continuity file, and seam-wave status. For reaction shots, lock visual speech semantics to the injected visibility: if all intersecting dialogue is off-screen and no visible person owns another on-screen event, no visible person may be described as speaking or opening their mouth to deliver the line. Subtitles and isolated open-mouth frames are not speaker evidence. Keep local detection warning-only, but do not merge a targeted rerun that still contains this contradiction.
10. Require the model to inventory every human visible in the original clip and evidence pages before asset matching. Keep unbound, tiny, blurred, edge, background, and middle-only people in `visible_characters`, naming unmatched people `背景路人A/B/C`; never place a scene or object in `visible_characters`. Compare every visible person's start/middle/end presence and describe a middle-only person as an observable enter/pass/occlude/exit action chain. For each cut/reframe, first record `screen_order_left_to_right` from the labelled evidence frame, then derive every person's `screen_position`, every pair's `horizontal_relation`, and the prose summary from that single order; never guess those fields independently. Also require `position_transition.type=stable|crosses_behind|crosses_in_front|reblocked_after_cut|unclear`, a visible transition description, and supporting timestamps. If the start/middle/end order reverses inside one record, describe the continuous crossing path. If the order changes across a cut, mark the following record `reblocked_after_cut`; never turn cut-based restaging into continuous movement. Use `person_trajectory` only to nominate these processes; never bind a track ID to a person name, infer facing, or override the video. After every cut or reframe, require a fresh horizontal `camera_view` judgment from the camera optical axis, character movement axis, and scene perspective vanishing point; keep vertical `camera_angle` and each character's torso/head orientation separate. A hard cut or three-quarter body pose does not by itself create a side view. Normalize structural labels only. The validator may warn about missing presence/view evidence or an obvious field/evidence contradiction, but warnings must not rewrite semantics, fail an otherwise valid range, or trigger an automatic relay rerun. Do not silently correct identity, action, orientation, dialogue, or cut decisions.
11. For an explicitly authorized full episode, merge complete ranges only after every planned range seam has a valid second-wave `continuity_decision`, segment each locked dialogue event across configured shot groups exactly once, and expand locally. After the final shot groups are fixed, run the deterministic continuity handoff writer across every adjacent final-group pair. It must write exactly one `continuity_handoff` onto the right-hand target group, classify it as exactly one of `inherit_state`, `new_scene`, or `verify`, and publish the same unique objects in root `continuity_handoffs`. For `N` final groups require exactly `N-1` handoffs (for the current 14-group episode, all 13 boundaries). Validate uniqueness, ownership, adjacent IDs, boundary time, state payloads, daylight, dialogue ownership, timing, and delivery before Excel export. Render one and only one `上一组尾态→本组首态` instruction inside each target row's `画面内容`; the first group has none. Build `scene_rules` from scene rows in the current project's British asset workbook, including the exact asset name, source/localized aliases, discriminating visible keywords, and negative keywords. Treat each configured group `scene` as a candidate, not evidence. Resolve it against each selected record's `scene_observation` and `fixed_scene_evidence`; correct a conflict only when one exact scene asset has a unique per-record win, at least two supporting records, and at least 67% of the group's visible duration. Preserve ambiguous conflicts with an audit warning (or fail in explicit `error` mode), and export `scene_resolution` metadata; never let a static group label silently suppress contrary video evidence. A hard cut, group boundary, dialogue boundary, or continuity injection alone does not prove a scene change. When a person matches an exact person-wardrobe/state asset, keep that complete asset identifier but omit repeated clothing colour, material, style, and accessory prose. When a scene matches an exact scene asset, omit repeated fixed décor, furniture, wall, material, and permanent-setting prose; retain only shot-specific lighting, action, blocking, focus, camera changes, and transient plot objects. When locked dialogue ranges are mixed with locally retained no-dialogue ranges, preserve the complete locked `delivery` object by `dialogue_id`; a retained range without a dialogue contract must not downgrade the merge. When a segment has no internal pause, retain the source `rhythm_pause` description and add the no-pause note separately. Keep the localized dialogue cell as pure speakable text; place non-spoken performance direction in the structured delivery field and the sound/dialogue prompt. A one-range canary must remain a separate partial comparison artifact.

For final-group handoffs, classify from the previous group's last resolved record scene and the target group's first resolved record scene, never by comparing whole-group composite strings. Require composite scene labels to follow temporal order: use the first component only as the unresolved entry fallback and the last component only as the unresolved exit fallback. A composite label such as `scene A；scene B` describes internal group coverage and does not by itself prove that its entry boundary changed scene. If an overlap audit returns `verify_from_video` while also declaring `same_scene_cut|continuous_action` and all of `scene_match`, `action_continues`, and `character_state_continues` true, normalize only the derived handoff to `inherit_state` and preserve the original audit object unchanged. Otherwise keep `verify` conservative.

Build `people_rules` from every exact episode person-state asset, including supporting attendants, doctors, staff, and other speaking roles, rather than only principal characters. When the locked ledger speaker is unclear, bind a speaker locally only when one exact current-episode person asset is uniquely supported by an `on_screen` dialogue placement plus matching visible-character action text (or the sole visible matched person); otherwise keep `unclear`.

Keep complete pairwise `relative_blocking` in structured compact JSON, but cap generation prose to the highest-value occlusion, depth, facing, crossing, and known horizontal relations with short evidence. Preserve screen-order and position-transition timelines. Retain source `on_screen_text` in analysis JSON, but exclude subtitles, identity graphics, platform warnings, watermarks, and other source-region overlays from generation prose. Emit readable text only through an explicit British-localized text rule; otherwise rely on the fixed no-text suffix.

For each seam injection, include the prior scene and daylight state, action/final/contact handoff, principal character posture/orientation/gaze/contact state, screen order, transient prop state, and unresolved persistent state objects from earlier records in the source group. This includes blindfold/eye-veil states and bedding/blanket contact states even when the source group's final frame summary no longer shows them. Do not attach or reference a real tail-frame image. Keep the left-only injection at `continuity_mode=verify_from_video` and `seam_type=pending_target_video_verification`; it must not guess the right-hand range. Require the second-wave seam audit to classify `seam_type=continuous_action|same_scene_cut|scene_transition|time_jump|unclear` and `continuity_mode=inherit_state|new_scene|verify_from_video` from overlap pixels. The seam audit may append this continuity decision to the transport audit but may not rewrite shot semantics.

The default five-phase execution contract is mandatory: (1) parse all planned ranges concurrently; (2) generate and audit all adjacent planned-range seams concurrently; (3) after final groups are known, deterministically write the audited/derived result to every right-hand target group; (4) merge/export a single `上一组尾态→本组首态` field per target group; and (5) fail validation unless every final-group boundary has exactly one `inherit_state`, `new_scene`, or `verify` result. Transport seam audits and final-group handoffs are different boundary layers and must both be complete.

Apply the five-phase contract independently inside every episode. Multiple episodes may occupy different phases simultaneously, but no episode may enter seam audit before all its own primary ranges validate, and no episode may merge before all its own transport seams validate. Keep local media concurrency, upload concurrency, and relay concurrency as separate limits; never derive local FFmpeg workers from relay slots.

## Evidence authority

Use this priority: clipped original video and audio, record-level `scene_observation` and `fixed_scene_evidence`, unmarked action and shot-space pages, shot-bounded person trajectories, then pose overlays. The expansion config's scene label and summaries are planning hints below direct video-derived scene evidence. Track IDs are valid only inside one supplied shot partition. Local evidence may trigger an attachment but must not assign identity, torso/head screen direction, gaze, facing relationship, physical depth, action meaning, or dialogue.

For every range, call `assemble_adaptive_evidence_pack.py` with both `--trajectory-page` and `--trajectory-json`. The relay runner rejects a current evidence pack that omits `person_trajectory`.
